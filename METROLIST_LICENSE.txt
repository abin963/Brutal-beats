This application incorporates architecture and features adapted from Metrolist
(https://github.com/vfsfitvnm/ViMusic / Metrolist), an open-source Android music player
licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

Adapted components include:
- AndroidX Media3 ExoPlayer & MediaSessionService architecture
- InnerTube YouTube Music parser and stream extractor
- Synchronized lyrics parser (LRCLIB format)
- Offline audio download cache patterns
